## The standard aimed at

The website aims at the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. This is an aim of the design and of the build, not a statement of conformance: no independent audit of the site is recorded in the repository, and this page does not claim that every success criterion is met.

## Measures built into the site

| Area | Measure |
|---|---|
| Structure | a skip link, shown on keyboard focus, leads from the top of every page to the content; the pages use landmarks (header, navigation, main region and footer) |
| Keyboard | the navigation, the menu of narrow screens (closed with the Escape key), the search, the copy buttons and the theme toggle can be operated from the keyboard |
| Focus | the keyboard focus is visible, drawn as a 2 px outline in the strong accent of the theme; the contrast of that outline is stated below |
| Colour | the light and the dark theme use the colours of the identity; the contrast values are in the table below |
| Text | the text can be resized in the browser |
| Scripts | the pages can be read without JavaScript, and the main navigation and the footer links work without it; JavaScript is used by the search, the copy buttons, the theme toggle, the menu button of narrow screens, the marking of the current heading in the table of contents and the motion of the pages |
| Motion | entrances, counters, the terminal session and the three-dimensional mark run only when the reader has not asked for reduced motion; otherwise the same content is shown at rest |
| Themes | the theme follows the preference of the system until you choose one with the toggle |

### Contrast of the identity colours

The contrast ratios were measured by the identity (WCAG 2.1 formula) and are stated in the identity document of the brand.

| Colours | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Silver `#DDE1E6` on graphite `#0B0C0E` | 14.9 : 1 | text on dark grounds |
| Gold `#C9A15A` on graphite `#0B0C0E` | 8.1 : 1 | accents on dark grounds |
| Ink `#2B2F36` on paper `#F6F7F8` | 12.5 : 1 | text on light grounds |
| Gold deep `#A3722A` on paper `#F6F7F8` | 3.9 : 1 | marks and lines only, never text |
| Gold ground `#D9BC85` on graphite `#0B0C0E` | 10.7 : 1 | the focus outline on the dark theme |

Gold deep stays below 4.5 : 1, so the site does not use it for text. The focus outline uses gold ground `#D9BC85` on the dark theme (10.7 : 1) and gold deep `#A3722A` on the light theme (3.9 : 1); both are above the 3 : 1 that success criterion 1.4.11 (non-text contrast) sets for the visible state of interface components. The two outline ratios were computed for this page with the WCAG formula from the colour tokens of the site. The other values are those of the identity; the site pages were not re-measured one by one for this page.

## Known limitations

- **The documentation is imported.** The pages under `/docs/` are rendered from the Markdown of the repository and keep its structure. Their headings, tables and link texts follow the writing rules of the documentation, not a separate accessibility review.
- **Code blocks may need horizontal scrolling.** Long lines of programs, commands and tables are not wrapped, and a narrow screen or a large text size can require scrolling sideways.
- **English only.** The documentation is available in English only. The pages written for the site are available in English and in Polish.
- **Images.** The renders and mockups of the identity carry a text alternative that describes the picture, but the pictures of the brand page are visual by nature, and the text next to them states the values they show (colours, sizes and rules).
- **Without JavaScript.** On a narrow screen the main menu is shown open under the header instead of behind a button. The search, the copy buttons and the theme switch are unavailable.
- **No audit.** No test with a screen reader on all pages and no external audit is recorded. Barriers may exist that the measures above do not prevent.

## Reporting a barrier

If you cannot use a page, a component or a document of the site, write to `support@danaco-group.pl`. Give the address of the page, what you tried to do, what stopped you, and the browser, the operating system and any assistive technology you use. The repository does not state a response time for such reports, and this page does not promise one. Reports about the program (not the site) follow [Support and contact](/project/support/).

The statement of what the site stores and sends is on the [privacy](/legal/privacy/) page; the identity colours are on the [brand](/project/brand/) page.
