## The standard aimed at

The website aims at the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. The aim shapes the design and the build: the structure of the pages, the operation from the keyboard, the colours of both themes, the motion and the behaviour without scripts follow it.

## Measures built into the site

Every page has the same structure. A skip link, shown on keyboard focus, leads from the top of the page to the content. The pages use landmarks: a header, a navigation, a main region and a footer. Headings follow the outline of the content, and the table of contents of a long page links to its headings.

The navigation, the menu of narrow screens, the search, the copy buttons and the theme toggle are operated from the keyboard, and the Escape key closes the menu. The text can be resized in the browser. The site follows the colour preference of the system until the reader chooses a theme with the toggle.

| Area | Measure |
|---|---|
| Structure | a skip link, shown on keyboard focus, leads from the top of every page to the content; the pages use landmarks (header, navigation, main region and footer) |
| Keyboard | the navigation, the menu of narrow screens (closed with the Escape key), the search, the copy buttons and the theme toggle can be operated from the keyboard |
| Focus | the keyboard focus is visible, drawn as a 2 px outline in the strong accent of the theme; the contrast of that outline is stated below |
| Colour | the light and the dark theme use the colours of the identity; the contrast values are in the table below |
| Text | the text can be resized in the browser |
| Scripts | the pages can be read without JavaScript, and the main navigation and the footer links work without it; JavaScript is used by the search, the copy buttons, the theme toggle, the menu button of narrow screens, the marking of the current heading in the table of contents and the motion of the pages |
| Motion | entrances, counters, the terminal session and the three-dimensional mark run when the reader has not asked for reduced motion; otherwise the same content is shown at rest |
| Themes | the theme follows the preference of the system until the reader chooses one with the toggle |

## Focus and contrast

The keyboard focus is drawn as an outline of 2 px. On the dark theme the outline is gold ground `#D9BC85` on graphite `#0B0C0E`, with a contrast of 10.7 : 1. On the light theme it is gold deep `#A3722A` on paper `#F6F7F8`, with a contrast of 3.9 : 1. Both values are above the 3 : 1 that success criterion 1.4.11 (non-text contrast) sets for the visible state of interface components.

### Contrast of the identity colours

The palette of the identity is described on the [identity](/design/identity/) page and in the [design](/design/) section.

| Colours | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Silver `#DDE1E6` on graphite `#0B0C0E` | 14.9 : 1 | text on dark grounds |
| Gold `#C9A15A` on graphite `#0B0C0E` | 8.1 : 1 | accents on dark grounds |
| Ink `#2B2F36` on paper `#F6F7F8` | 12.5 : 1 | text on light grounds |
| Gold deep `#A3722A` on paper `#F6F7F8` | 3.9 : 1 | marks and lines |
| Gold ground `#D9BC85` on graphite `#0B0C0E` | 10.7 : 1 | the focus outline on the dark theme |

Text uses the pairs of 4.5 : 1 and above: silver on graphite and ink on paper. Gold deep serves marks and lines.

## Motion

Entrances of elements, counters, the terminal session and the three-dimensional mark run when the reader has not asked for reduced motion. With the setting for reduced motion the same content appears at rest, with no movement.

## Behaviour without scripts

The pages are readable without JavaScript. The main navigation and the footer links work without it. On a narrow screen the main menu is shown open under the header. The search, the copy buttons and the theme toggle use JavaScript.

## Reporting a barrier

A reader who cannot use a page, a component or a document of the site writes to `support@danaco-group.pl`. The message names the address of the page, what the reader tried to do, what stopped the reader, and the browser, the operating system and the assistive technology in use. Reports about the program (not the site) follow [Support and contact](/project/support/).

The statement of what the site stores and sends is on the [privacy](/legal/privacy/) page; the identity colours are on the [identity](/design/identity/) page.
