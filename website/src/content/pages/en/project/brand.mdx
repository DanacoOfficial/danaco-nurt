## The mark

<BrandImage name="render-hero-front" alt="The mark of Danaco Nurt as a metal object: a silver chevron on the left and a gold chevron on the right, rendered on a dark ground." width={1200} caption="Render of the mark, production identity 1.0.0." />

The mark has two chevrons: a silver `<` that steps back and a gold `>` that steps forward. The gold chevron is the silver one turned by 180 degrees about the centre of a 96 x 96 grid. A change and its undo are one figure. The channel between the parallel arms is the stream, in Polish *nurt*, the name of the product.

The production identity 1.0.0 is dated 2026-11-26. It keeps the idea and the silhouette of the earlier mark and corrects its optics:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Grid | 96 x 96 units, centre of the turn (48, 48) |
| Slope of the arms | 3 : 4 (36.87 degrees) |
| Stroke | 11 units |
| Stream between the chevrons | 5.5 units, equal along the band |
| Cuts of the arms | vertical, x = 49.7 and x = 46.3; the tips interlock by 3.4 units, so no vertical line divides the figure |
| Box of the mark | 67.0 x 77.28 units |

The paths of both chevrons are in `identity.json`. The identity document states that the figure still overlaps a plain `<>` pair by an index of 0.199: the interlock of the tips reduces the association with brackets and does not remove it.

## Colour

The tokens are in `tokens.css` and `identity.json`; the names describe the function.

| Role | Token | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Ground, main | `graphite` | `#0B0C0E` |
| Ground, soft | `graphite_soft` | `#15171A` |
| Ground, deep black | `black_deep` | `#050506` |
| Step back | `silver` | `#DDE1E6` |
| Step forward | `gold` | `#C9A15A` |
| Ink on light | `ink` | `#2B2F36` |
| Gold on light | `gold_deep` | `#A3722A` |
| Ground, light | `paper` | `#F6F7F8` |
| Ground, warm light | `paper_warm` | `#F2EEE6` |
| Ground, gold | `gold_ground` | `#D9BC85` |
| Pipe `\|>` in the terminal and in code | cyan | `#4FD1F5` |

Contrast, measured by the identity (WCAG 2.1): silver on graphite 14.9 : 1, gold on graphite 8.1 : 1, ink on paper 12.5 : 1, and gold deep on paper 3.9 : 1, which is used for marks and lines only and never as text. Cyan is reserved for the pipe and strings in the terminal and is not used in the logo. CMYK recipes and Pantone candidates are in the identity document; the Pantone entries are candidates, to be confirmed at the first print.

## Typefaces

The name is set in Geist, SIL Open Font License: Danaco in weight 400 and Nurt in weight 700, so that the company name steps back and the product name steps forward. The letters N, r and t carry a cut at the slope of the arms of the mark. Geist Mono is the typeface of the terminal and of code. In every file of the identity the lettering is converted to curves.

## Minimum sizes and clear space

The mark below 48 px uses separate drawings aligned to the pixel grid: S16 for 16 to 23 px, S24 for 24 to 31 px and S32 for 32 to 47 px. The main drawing starts at 48 px and is never scaled below it.

| Layout | Minimum on screen | Minimum in ink |
|---|---|---|
| Mark | 16 px (S16), 24 px (S24), 32 px (S32), main drawing from 48 px | 6 mm |
| Horizontal lockup | 96 px wide | 28 mm |
| Vertical lockup | 64 px | 20 mm |
| Compact lockup | 56 px | 20 mm |
| Wordmark | 72 px | 22 mm |

Hot foil needs a horizontal lockup of at least 40.8 mm and a blind emboss a mark at least 19.3 mm high. Clear space around the mark alone is a quarter of the height of its box; around a lockup it is the cap height of the wordmark on every side.

## Rules of use

1. Use the supplied files; do not redraw or retype the mark or the name.
2. On a dark ground use `color-dark` or `white`; on a light ground `color-light` or `black`.
3. The silver chevron is always left and raised and the gold chevron is right and lowered. The tones are never swapped.
4. Gold in tint appears only as gold deep (`#A3722A`) on light grounds, never as text on `#F6F7F8`.
5. A variant is allowed on a ground only when the mark keeps 3 : 1 and the lettering 4.5 : 1 against it.

Forbidden uses: swapping the tones of the chevrons; stretching or squeezing; mirroring (the figure is turned, not reflected); outlining; shadows and glow; colour on a ground that keeps less than 3 : 1; busy or coloured backgrounds; rotation; another typeface for the name; changing the angle or the stroke; crowding the clear space; gradients that are not in the system.
