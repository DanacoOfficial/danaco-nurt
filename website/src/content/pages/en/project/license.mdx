## Third-party dependencies

All dependencies are declared in `[workspace.dependencies]`, are taken from crates.io only and are checked by `cargo deny --locked check` against the policy in [deny.toml](https://github.com/DanacoOfficial/danaco-nurt/blob/release-0.1.2/deny.toml). The policy:

- **Allowed licenses:** `0BSD`, `Apache-2.0`, `Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception`, `BSD-2-Clause`, `BSD-3-Clause`, `BSL-1.0`, `CC0-1.0`, `ISC`, `MIT`, `MIT-0`, `Unicode-3.0`, `Unlicense` and `Zlib`. A dependency under an expression with alternatives is accepted when one alternative is allowed.
- **Exceptions for single crates:** `CDLA-Permissive-2.0` for `webpki-roots`, `bzip2-1.0.6` for `libbz2-rs-sys`, `MPL-2.0` for the unmodified transitive crates `uluru` and `option-ext`, and `NCSA` for `libfuzzer-sys`, which is linked only into fuzzing targets and never into distributed artifacts.
- **Excluded:** GPL, LGPL and AGPL in every version, and licenses that restrict the field of use. The product does not embed or link code of GPL or LGPL programs. `deny.toml` bans, among others, `sequoia-openpgp`, `openssl`, `git2` and `unrar`, each with its reason.
- **Advisories:** one advisory is ignored, RUSTSEC-2026-0543 (`chrono` is unmaintained and has no reported vulnerability), with the reason recorded in `deny.toml`.

The [dependencies](/architecture/dependencies/) page and [supply chain](/quality/supply-chain/) describe the same policy from the side of the build.

## Names and marks

The names "Danaco", "Danaco Nurt" and "Danaco Holding Group" are names of Danaco Holding Group Sp. z o.o. Section 6 of the Apache License does not grant permission to use them, except for the customary description of the origin of the product and the reproduction of the `NOTICE` file. The naming conventions add:

- a descriptive statement such as "works with Danaco Nurt" is customary use;
- a modified version of the program must not be distributed under the name Danaco Nurt without the permission of Danaco Holding Group Sp. z o.o.;
- the executable is `nurt` (`nurt.exe` on Windows), and no other names or aliases are installed.

The name "Danaco Nurt Compatible" and the mark of the program may be used by an implementation or an integration that passes the mandatory tests of `nurt-conformance`. The documentation states that the terms of this program are published after the owner of the name approves them; they are not published yet in the sources this site was written from. Visual identity files are described on the [brand](/project/brand/) page. Sources: [naming conventions](/docs/conventions/naming/) and [compatibility](/docs/guide/compatibility/).
