## The creator

Danaco Nurt was designed and developed by Dariusz Naharnowicz.

> I did not want another shell. I wanted a language made for the age of AI. A language that gives full awareness of changes before they are made, full transparency after they are made, and full control over undoing each of them. When I did not find such a solution, I designed it from scratch.
>
> Dariusz Naharnowicz, creator of Danaco Nurt

## What it is

Danaco Nurt is a command shell and a language where every change can be undone, built for people and AI agents. The program is `nurt`, pronounced *NOORT*. The design rests on five points:

- one executable per operating system, for Linux, macOS and Windows;
- file, text, archive, document, code, version control, network and system operations built into the program;
- programs as pipelines of typed records, checked against the command catalog before any effect takes place;
- a structured report of the effects of every run;
- a journal of file changes that can be reviewed and undone.

The engine is a Rust library with a C interface. The repository also holds bindings for Python, Go, Node, Java and .NET, integrations for editors and containers, a conformance suite and benchmarks. The [overview](/product/overview/) and the [architecture](/architecture/overview/) pages describe them.

## The origin of the name

*Nurt* is a Polish word for a stream or a current. The mark shows it: two chevrons, a silver one that steps back and a gold one that steps forward, are separated by a channel between their parallel arms, and that channel is the stream, in Polish *nurt*, the name of the product. The mark is also the figure of the main idea of the product: the second chevron is the first turned by 180 degrees, as an undo turns a change.

The [design](/design/) section presents the visual identity: the mark, the colours and the guidelines of their use. The page [Identity](/design/identity/) holds the guidelines.
